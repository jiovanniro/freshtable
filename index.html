Why we built this

"I kept my HelloFresh recipe cards after I cancelled. The recipes were great — I just didn't want to pay for the groceries twice. But then I was building my shopping lists by hand, running out of new ideas, and my other meal app kept adding the same ingredient over and over every time I planned two meals at once. How it works

Three steps to a better dinner week

1

Pick your recipes

Browse a curated library with new AI-generated recipes added every week. Or import your existing recipe cards from a photo or PDF — HelloFresh cards, cookbooks, anything.

New recipes weekly
2

Build your meal plan

Add meals to your week with a tap. Mix and match recipes across the library. No limits, no preset boxes — just what you actually want to cook.

3

Get your smart list

FreshTable consolidates ingredients across all your recipes automatically. If two meals need chicken, it shows up once with the right total quantity. Check off what you already have — no pantry setup required.
